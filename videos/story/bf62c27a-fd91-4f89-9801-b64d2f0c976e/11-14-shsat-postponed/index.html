11/14 SHSAT Postponed

NOTE: This is the first year of the digital ADAPTIVE test, and the second year of the test being administered digitally. TNN reporter Alex Wolfowitz examines what went wrong during this morning's SHSAT after a technical issue with the testing platform forced the NYC Department of Education to halt testing. Some students completed the exam; others were sent home. Disclaimer: TNN has confirmed these issues for Brooklyn Tech and Stuyvesant, and there are unconfirmed reports of technical issues at other testing sites across the city.
