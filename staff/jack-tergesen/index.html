Jack Tergesen

Head Anchor, Script Editor, Executive Producer, Chief Meteorologist, Marketer, Script Writer & Director

Jack Tergesen (he/him) is a senior in the Social Science Research Major. Jack founded TNN because he has had a lifelong passion for communication and aspires to potentially work as a broadcast meteorologist. Jack believes that valuing an audience is key and has built TNN with that ideology, ensuring that stories are told through a dynamic, interesting, and engaging lens. Outside of TNN, he enjoys co-leading the school newspaper, The Survey, rock climbing, and going out in the city with his friends! Jack is excited to continue TNN's partnership with Columbia and produce new segments.
