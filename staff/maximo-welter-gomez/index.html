Máximo Welter-Gomez

Script Writer & Anchor

Máximo Welter-Gomez (he/him) is a junior in the Social Science Research major. He joined TNN in his freshman year, because he had always had an interest in news and news production but couldn’t seem to fully get into the groove of writing articles for the Survey. When provided the opportunity to help start and join TNN, he jumped at the chance and has loved it ever since. In his free time, Máximo writes and produces music, reads, and watches Real Madrid. He believes that good journalism is one of the most important factors in keeping a healthy and stable modern society.
